Heron flies the Dry Canal District

A gameplay mockup. Heron is about three tiles tall, roughly a four-storey building, in a ruined city at night, lit with teal shadow and warm light. It never walks: it skims the street on its thrusters or takes off and flies. Use WASD or the arrow keys to boost, Shift to quick boost, Space to take off or land, or click or tap to fly there. It patrols on its own when left alone.

Scale

The view is 1024 × 576 native pixels over a 120 × 90 tile city, and the screen fills the browser width. Everything is drawn at about 4.6 pixels per metre: Heron is about 46 pixels (10 m), a storey is 15 pixels, a car is about 22, a street lamp about 28, and a four-lane road 64.

Teal dark, warm light

Every pixel is multiplied by a teal ambient light map. Only lamps, fires, lit windows and Heron's sensor add warm or cold light back, in stepped pixel bands rather than smooth gradients.

Torso first, legs follow

Heron has 16 frames per direction. Standing, it drops into the angled stance from the structure study, turned three-quarters to the camera with its feet staggered and the rifle held low, and it breathes: the hips dip and the free arm sways. Moving, the torso and the legs are tracked separately on a 3 × 3 grid of poses: the torso tips forward and the rifle comes level first, then the legs sweep back as speed builds. In flight the legs stay swept back and still. A hard turn swings the torso round before the legs catch up, and letting go throws the feet forward to brake.

Fast

Boosting tops out around 275 km/h on the gauge, flight is a quarter faster, and a quick boost bursts past 600. Past about 300 you get afterimages and speed lines, and the camera leads ahead of Heron so you can see where you're going. Flying clears cars, lamps and rubble, but not buildings.

Diorama layers

The ground, the buildings and Heron are sorted by depth. Cloud shadows drift over everything, fog moves at a slower parallax, and dark girders and cables pass in front at a faster parallax, so the scene reads like a shallow box.

Everything here is original, drawn by code and the mech kit. The map is generated from a fixed seed, so it's the same city on every visit.
